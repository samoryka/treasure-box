  <p>
  I wanted a bed that met two criteria: it had to look simple and be made of wood (i.e. not particle board).
  </p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  I thus built this one! It was challenging to align the pieces properly, and thrilling to see whether it would hold my weight. So far, it has, and after two years I still trust it!
</p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  I didn't think much about how I'd transport it, so it isn't easy to disassemble. Let's see what the future holds if I move...
</p>
