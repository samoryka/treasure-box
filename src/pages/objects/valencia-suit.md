<section>
  <p>
     I attended the Gay Games with my <a href="https://www.berlinaquaholics.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">waterpolo team</a> in 2026. They took place in Valencia. With the far right gaining power there, we wanted to do something to support the local community. We decided to sell waterpolo swimsuits to raise funds for a local association: <a href="https://lambdavalencia.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lambda Valencia</a>. We ended up raising 700€!
  </p>
</section>

<section>
  <p>
    I came up with the design. It mixes different elements from the <a href="https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escut_de_Val%C3%A8ncia" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Valencian coat of arms</a> and colors from the trans flag. It's meant to anchor trans & queer people in a land that's theirs just like anyone else's.
  </p>
</section>
