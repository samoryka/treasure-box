<section>
  <p>
  After seeing a video explaining the concept of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smocking" target="_blank" rel="noopener">smocking</a>, I decided to try it for myself. It pushed me to make this first lamp!
  </p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  For the base I made a cube out of plywood and applied two coats of hard wax oil on it. I could reuse a lamp cable I had lying around.
</p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  The lampshade is the part that uses smocking. I first created a simple lampshade using a lampshade frame, lampshade adhesive, and some cotton fabric. I could find them all at <a href="https://www.modulor.de/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">modulor</a>.
</p>
<p>
  I then smocked a piece of the same fabric. I ended up needing to really stretch it on the lampshade, but it was a happy accident. It revealed the pattern more!
</p>
<p>
  I could finally glue that smocked fabric on the lamp and add some bias tape on the sides.
</p>
</section>
