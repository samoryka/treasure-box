<section>
  <p>
  I love watching videos of people talking about how they set up their living spaces. I once stumbled upon <a href="https://www.nevertoosmall.com/post/hidden-garden-house-sydney" target="_blank" rel="noopener">this one</a>, where a couple talks about their house in Sydney.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  I noticed their table and thought it could be a good solution for the studio I was living in at the time. I wanted a table big enough to be a desk most days and a dining table when I have guests.
</p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  I built it out of pine and used pocket holes and glue to assemble everything.
</p>
<p>
  It has had a few lives. I used it in my later home as a dining table only. After a while I shortened it and it became my work surface for woodworking, as I don't mind it getting beat up with time.
</p>
