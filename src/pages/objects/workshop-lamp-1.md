<section>
  <p>
  I was about to attend a (wonderful) party where anyone can offer a workshop. I decided to share the lamp-making skills I learned and came up with this model that could be replicated by anyone.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  I prepared the lamp bases in advance. The goal was to have people make their own lampshades. They could use pieces of fabric they had lying around, old clothes, etc. One just needs to lay out some PVC squares, make some folds, and glue some parts. The outcome is a freestanding cubic lampshade that just needs to be tucked between the three legs of the base.
</p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  Since then, I've run the workshop a few times, and it's always very fun! When time allows, we can also oil the wooden bases together.
</p>
</section>
