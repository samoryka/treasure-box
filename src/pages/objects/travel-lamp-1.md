<section>
  <p>
  Two of my friends moved to another country, and I wanted to make something they could bring with them. That gave me the idea for a lamp they could easily travel with.
  </p>
</section>

<section>
<p>
  I used handmade joinery to create a structure that could easily be disassembled. The lampshade is held neatly by the wooden structure, so no metal frame is needed.
</p>
</section>
